Colour Partner

Tap a swatch to copy its hex. Long-press (or double-click) a swatch to make it your new base colour. Preview shows a scheme full screen.

Light (RGB) is the additive wheel your fixtures and console use — red's complement is cyan. Paint (RYB) is the traditional art-school wheel — red's complement is green. Hue degrees shown are always the RGB/console hue.

RGBW moves the part all three channels share onto the white emitter. CMY is for subtractive fixtures with colour flags: 100% = flag fully in.

Works offline once loaded. Add it to your home screen to use it like an app.
